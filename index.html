FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee profits?

No. Trading can lose money, including with a well-tested EA. Backtests are historical simulations and don't predict future results.

Are you a broker? Do you manage accounts?

No. Smartbox doesn't accept deposits, hold client funds or trade on your behalf. You choose your own broker and stay in control of your account.

Do you guarantee I'll pass a prop firm challenge?

No. We help you understand the rules and plan your risk. Passing and funding depend on your trading and the firm's decision.

What is an Expert Advisor?

An Expert Advisor (EA) is a program that runs in MetaTrader and follows coded rules to analyse the market and manage orders. It still needs testing, sensible settings and monitoring.

Can I request a custom EA or indicator?

Yes. Send your rules, platform and market. We'll tell you whether it's feasible and give you a written scope and quote before any payment.

How does mentorship work?

Topics and format are agreed around your level. Sessions focus on analysis, risk management and reviewing your own trades. It's education, not personal investment advice.

Do I need a VPS?

Usually only if an EA must run while your computer is off. A VPS improves availability but can't guarantee uninterrupted running.

What is copy trading?

It automatically mirrors another trader's strategy on your account. Their losses can be copied too, and past results don't predict future ones.

Where do the prices and news come from?

Prices, charts and the calendar are provided by TradingView and may be delayed. Headlines come from public news feeds and link to the original publisher. Smartbox doesn't write or endorse them.
